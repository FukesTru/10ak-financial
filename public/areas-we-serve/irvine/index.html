What is different about planning your finances in Irvine?

The city of Irvine was planned from the start, and its neighborhoods are called villages. Woodbridge, Northwood, Turtle Rock, University Park, Quail Hill and Portola Springs each have their own parks, schools and shops. The 5 and the 405 cross the city and meet near the Irvine Spectrum area. The 133, 241 and 261 toll roads connect it with the hills to the east.

Jobs gather in a few places. The Irvine Spectrum area holds tech, medical device and life sciences offices. The business district near John Wayne Airport has finance, legal and corporate offices. UC Irvine and its research park bring in faculty, researchers and graduate students. People who work in these places may get part of their pay in company stock.

Owning a home here can come with costs you may not see elsewhere. Many newer neighborhoods sit inside a Community Facilities District, often called Mello-Roos. That district adds a special tax to the property tax bill to pay for things like roads, schools and parks. Most villages also have homeowners association dues. Both belong in any budget for buying a home here.

Which services do Irvine residents ask about most?

Tax Planning

Restricted stock units, or RSUs, are shares your employer gives you once you reach a vesting date set in your grant. They are taxed as wages when they vest, and the tax withheld may not cover your full bill. We plan for that gap well before April.

Tax-Free Investment

A Roth IRA is a retirement account where qualified withdrawals are tax-free. If your income is too high to add to one directly, a backdoor Roth may still work. That means putting after-tax money into a traditional IRA, the standard kind of individual retirement account, and then converting it. Other IRA balances can change the tax, so we check first.

Questions

What do people in Irvine ask us?

Why do I owe tax in April when my RSUs were already taxed?

When shares vest, your employer withholds tax, often at a flat rate set for extra pay like bonuses. If your actual tax rate is higher than that flat rate, the withholding falls short. California taxes the same income too. Our tax planning estimates the gap after each vest so you can set money aside or adjust your withholding.

What should I do with shares from an ESPP?

An employee stock purchase plan, or ESPP, lets you buy company stock through payroll, often at a discount. How a sale is taxed depends on how long you hold the shares after the purchase and after the offering date. Owning too much of one company also adds risk. We plan a selling schedule with both tax and risk in mind.

Is Mello-Roos tax deductible on my Irvine home?

Sometimes, at least in part. Special taxes that pay for local improvements are often treated differently from regular property tax. The answer depends on how your district sets the charge, so we read your tax bill with you line by line before claiming anything.

Does a 529 plan make sense if my child might attend UC Irvine?

Money in a 529 plan grows without tax as long as it pays for qualified education costs. It can cover tuition, books and housing at UC Irvine and most other colleges. California does not give a state tax deduction for 529 deposits, so the benefit comes from tax-free growth. Our college planning compares it with other ways to save.
