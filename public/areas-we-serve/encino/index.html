Services

How can 1Oak help an Encino practice or a retired couple?

Pension and Cash Balance Plans

A cash balance plan is a pension the business funds for you, with yearly limits that rise with age. For a practice owner in their 50s or 60s with steady profits, it may allow far larger tax-deferred savings than a 401(k) alone. Tax-deferred means you pay the income tax later, when you take the money out.

Read more

Annuities and Rollovers

An annuity, bought from an insurance company, can pay you income for life or for a set number of years. Some retirees use part of an IRA rollover, which moves money from a work plan into an IRA, to buy one. Those payments rely on the insurer's ability to pay claims.

Read more

Advanced Financial Ratio Analysis

Before adding a partner, buying your office condo or selling the practice, it helps to see how the business performs over time. A ratio is one number divided by another, such as overhead compared to collections. We track ratios like that and profit per provider across several years.

Los Angeles County

Who lives and works along Ventura Boulevard in Encino?

Encino belongs to the City of Los Angeles and sits along the south edge of the San Fernando Valley in Los Angeles County. Ventura Boulevard runs through its center, lined with medical buildings, dental offices, law offices and professional suites. The 101 runs along the north side of the neighborhood, and the 405 passes on the east side as it climbs the Sepulveda Pass toward the Westside.

South of the boulevard, hillside streets rise toward Mulholland Drive. North of it, flatter streets lead toward the Sepulveda Basin. That large public open space holds Lake Balboa, sports fields, bike paths and a stretch of the Los Angeles River. Los Encinos State Historic Park, a small park just off Ventura Boulevard, keeps some of the area's early history.

Many households here include a physician, dentist, therapist or attorney who owns a practice and is in peak earning years. Others are retirees or near-retirees who have saved for decades and now want that money to last. Because Encino falls inside Los Angeles city limits, a business here usually has to register with the city. It may also owe city business tax in addition to state and federal taxes.

Questions

What do people in Encino ask us?

Does a cash balance plan work for an Encino practice with staff?

It can, but your employees usually must be included and receive contributions too. The design often pairs a cash balance plan with a 401(k), so the owner can save more while keeping staff costs reasonable. Each year an actuary, someone trained in the math of pensions, works out the required contribution.

I am retiring soon. Should I roll my 401(k) into an annuity?

Maybe part of it. We look at your other income, such as Social Security and any pension, and see how much of your monthly costs are already covered. If a gap remains, one of the annuities and rollovers options may fill part of it while the rest stays invested. Before you decide, we explain the costs and surrender charges, which are penalties for taking money out early.

Does it matter that Encino sits inside Los Angeles city limits?

For a business, yes. The City of Los Angeles has its own business tax and registration, which a practice based in Encino usually must handle. The city also adds a transfer tax when higher priced property sells, which can affect a home or building sale, so we factor both into your plans.
