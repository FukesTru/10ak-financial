Services

Which services fit Beverly Hills property owners and families?

Cost Segregation Study

Depreciation lets you deduct the cost of a building a little at a time over many years. Say you own an apartment building south of Wilshire or an office building near Beverly Drive. A cost segregation study may let you take that deduction faster by splitting the building into parts with shorter write-off periods. California does not follow every federal depreciation rule, so we check both returns.

Read more

Life Insurance

The federal estate tax is a tax on the value of what someone leaves behind above a set amount. Buildings are hard to sell quickly, so life insurance can give heirs cash to pay that tax and other costs. Any payout rests on the insurer's ability to pay claims.

Read more

Tax-Free Investment

A Roth conversion moves money from a traditional IRA into a Roth IRA. You pay income tax on the amount now, and later qualified withdrawals, for you or your heirs, can come out without income tax. Doing it in a lower-income year may reduce the tax cost.

Read more

Strategic Risk Management

A large home, art, jewelry and a public profile can bring more risk than a standard policy covers. We review your home coverage, your umbrella policy, which is extra liability coverage above your home and auto limits, and whether valuable items are listed on their own.

Los Angeles County

What is different about planning in Beverly Hills?

Beverly Hills is its own city in Los Angeles County, surrounded mostly by the City of Los Angeles, with West Hollywood along part of its east side. No freeway runs through it. Wilshire Boulevard, Santa Monica Boulevard and Sunset Boulevard carry most of the traffic, and the 405 is a short drive west. The Golden Triangle, bounded by Wilshire, Santa Monica Boulevard and Cañon Drive, holds Rodeo Drive along with blocks of medical, legal and business offices.

North of Santa Monica Boulevard, the flats have larger homes on wide, tree-lined streets, and the hillside areas above Sunset have estate lots and canyon views. South of Wilshire, many blocks hold apartment buildings and duplexes, some kept by the same family for decades. Beverly Gardens Park stretches along Santa Monica Boulevard, and Greystone Mansion and Gardens, a city-owned estate, sits in the hills above.

For many households here, real estate makes up much of their net worth, meaning the value of everything they own after subtracting debts. That brings up questions about depreciation on rental buildings, how property passes to children and how heirs will cover taxes without a forced sale. Physicians, attorneys and business managers with offices in the Golden Triangle also tend to need year-round tax planning rather than a once-a-year return.

Questions

What do people in Beverly Hills ask us?

Does California have its own estate tax?

Not at this time. California does not charge a separate estate or inheritance tax, but the federal estate tax can apply to larger estates. The federal limit has changed several times, so we check the current rule each year.

What happens to the tax basis of a Beverly Hills building I leave to my children?

Basis is the amount the tax rules treat as your cost in an asset. Under current federal rules, inherited property usually gets a new basis equal to its value on the date of death. That can remove much of the taxable gain built up during your life. Community property is property a married couple owns together under California law, and both halves may get a new basis when the first spouse dies.

Can you work with my estate attorney and property manager?

Yes. We focus on the tax and money side, and we do not draft legal documents. With your permission, we share numbers with your attorney, property manager or bookkeeper so the plan fits together. If you own rentals, we can also tell you whether a cost segregation study is worth a closer look.
