Where is the 1Oak office in Monrovia?

Our office is at 635 W. Foothill Blvd, on the north side of Monrovia, where the streets begin to rise toward the San Gabriel Mountains. Old Town Monrovia, with its shops, cafes and restaurants along Myrtle Avenue, is a few minutes away. The 210 Foothill Freeway crosses the city to the south. You can exit at Myrtle Avenue and head north, or follow Foothill Boulevard in from Arcadia or Duarte.

Monrovia is a foothill city in Los Angeles County with a mix of old and new. Many streets are lined with Craftsman and Victorian homes, while newer houses sit higher up near Monrovia Canyon Park and its trails. South of the freeway, Huntington Drive has office buildings, warehouses and larger employers. On Friday evenings, a street fair brings families out to Myrtle Avenue.

Monrovia households are a varied group. Some people run a shop, salon or restaurant in Old Town. Others are contractors, landscapers and tradespeople, or consultants who work from a spare bedroom. Many families bought their homes decades ago and now think about retirement, college or helping a parent. Neighbors in Arcadia, Duarte, Bradbury and Sierra Madre are close by as well.

Services

Which services do Monrovia business owners and homeowners ask about?

Accounting and Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping means recording every sale, bill and payment so you know where your business stands each month. For an Old Town shop owner or a contractor running several jobs at once, current books make sales tax filings, loan requests and year-end taxes much easier.

Read more

Tax Return Filing

Plenty of Monrovia households have more to report than a paycheck, such as a side business, a rented unit behind the house or a parent's final return. We prepare your federal and California returns together and help you respond to letters from the IRS or the Franchise Tax Board, which collects state income tax.

Read more

Strategic Risk Management

Homes near Monrovia Canyon and the upper foothill streets sit close to brush that can burn. We look at what it would cost to rebuild and what your policy would pay. We also check how you would cover rent if you had to live elsewhere during repairs.

Read more

Tax Planning

Tax planning means reviewing your choices before the year ends, while they can still lower what you owe. For a local owner, that could mean timing an equipment purchase, starting a retirement plan for the business or choosing how to pay yourself.

Questions

What do people in Monrovia ask us?

Do I have to meet in person if I live in Monrovia?

No. Living close simply gives you the choice. You can come to the office for the first visit and switch to video or phone later. Or start online and come in when you need to sign something.

Can you help a small business in Old Town Monrovia?

Yes. We help shop, restaurant and service business owners with monthly bookkeeping, sales tax records, year-end returns and planning. If your books have fallen behind, we can catch up the past months first and then keep them current.

What should I bring to my first meeting at the Monrovia office?

Bring your last 2 years of tax returns, a recent pay stub, and any letters from tax agencies. Business owners should add a profit and loss report, which shows income and costs for a period. Homeowners can bring a mortgage statement and the summary page of their insurance policy. We explain fees at that meeting, before any work starts, and our fees page explains the approach ahead of time.

Do you also work with people in Duarte, Bradbury and Sierra Madre?

Yes. Those towns are a short drive from Foothill Boulevard, and meetings work the same way for you as they do for Monrovia residents. We also serve clients across California by video and phone.
