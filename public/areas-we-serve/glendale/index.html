Who lives and does business in Glendale?

Glendale is a city in Los Angeles County, set between Burbank, Pasadena and the Verdugo Mountains. The 134 crosses it from east to west, the 2 heads north toward La Cañada Flintridge, and the 5 runs along its western side. Downtown grew up around Brand Boulevard and Central Avenue, where office towers stand a block or so from restaurants, car showrooms and older storefronts.

Away from downtown, the city changes from one area to the next. Montrose has a walkable shopping street on Honolulu Avenue. Rossmoyne, Adams Hill and the Verdugo Woodlands are quiet hillside neighborhoods, and the north end reaches into the foothills toward La Crescenta. Brand Park and its library sit at the base of the mountains, and apartment and condo buildings fill many blocks south of the 134.

Many Glendale companies are owner-run. Restaurants, medical and dental offices, repair shops, law offices and real estate firms are often still led by their founders. Sometimes a son or daughter works alongside. Sooner or later the owner needs answers to 3 questions. What is the business worth, who will run it next, and can I retire on what it pays?

What do people in Glendale ask us?

When should a Glendale family business start succession planning?

Ideally 5 to 10 years before the owner wants to step back. That gives time to train the next leader, clean up the books and choose between a sale, a gift or a gradual buyout. Starting late still helps, though it leaves fewer choices.

Will a bank look at my business ratios before it lends?

Usually, yes. Banks often check how much debt your company carries and whether its cash flow covers the loan payments. Debt service coverage is the ratio of cash available to the loan payments due. Our financial ratio analysis lets you see these numbers and fix weak spots before you apply.

Can you work with the bookkeeper my Glendale company already has?

Yes. We can review the books they keep, suggest changes to how accounts are set up and build planning reports from that work. If you need full monthly bookkeeping, our accounting and bookkeeping service covers that too.
