Orange County

What is Orange County like for business owners and investors?

From Brea and Fullerton in the north to San Clemente in the south, Orange County is a long line of connected cities. Santa Ana is the county seat, with the county courts and government offices. Anaheim, Orange and Garden Grove fill the north and central parts, while Huntington Beach, Newport Beach and Laguna Beach line the coast. Mission Viejo, Lake Forest and Rancho Santa Margarita cover the south and east.

Most trips use a handful of main freeways. The 5 and the 405 run the length of the county. The 57 and the 55 run north and south through the middle, and the 91 and the 22 cross east and west. In the south and east, the 73, 241 and 261 toll roads serve the hills and the south county.

Owners across the county run medical and dental practices, contracting and trade firms, restaurants, shops and family companies that may pass to the next generation. Some households own rental homes or small commercial buildings bought years ago. Others are deciding whether to retire in California or move out of state.

Services

Which services do Orange County owners and investors ask about most?

Pension and Cash Balance Plans

Physicians, dentists and other owners with steady profits often ask about a cash balance plan. It is a type of pension the business funds, with yearly amounts based on age and pay. It can be added next to a 401(k) to raise total savings.

Read more

Cost Segregation Study

If you bought a rental or small commercial building years ago, a look-back cost segregation study may still help. Depreciation is the yearly tax deduction for wear on a property, and the study can catch up deductions you missed without amending old returns.

Read more

Advanced Financial Ratio Analysis

Family businesses preparing for a sale, a loan or a handoff to children can gain from knowing their numbers early. Ratios such as profit margin, the share of sales left as profit, show a buyer or lender how the company has done over time.

Tax Planning

If you plan to retire out of state, the order of steps matters. California can still tax some income tied to the state, and the Franchise Tax Board looks closely at moves. We help you plan the move, any property sales and your withdrawals.

How far is the drive from Orange County to Monrovia?

It depends on where you start. From Brea or Fullerton, the 57 north runs straight to the 210, and Monrovia is a short run west from there. That trip often takes 30 to 40 minutes outside rush hour. From the coast or south county, take the 405 or the 5 to the 605 north and stay on it until the 210. That drive can run 75 minutes or more. If I retire to another state, can California still tax my income?

In some cases, yes. Once you are a nonresident, California can still tax income from California sources, such as rent from a property here. Federal law generally keeps states from taxing a former resident's pension and most retirement account withdrawals. The Franchise Tax Board may review a move, so keep records that show where your home really is.

Can a cost segregation study help with a building I bought years ago?

It may. A look-back study finds depreciation you could have claimed in past years. A change in accounting method, filed with the IRS on a set form, can let you claim that missed amount on this year's return. Results depend on the building, its age and how you have used it.
