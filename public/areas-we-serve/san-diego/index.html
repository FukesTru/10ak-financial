San Diego County

Which parts of San Diego life affect your taxes and planning?

Bays, beaches and inland mesas give San Diego neighborhoods very different settings. In Downtown, the Gaslamp Quarter, East Village and Little Italy sit near the harbor. Balboa Park lies just north, with Hillcrest and North Park beyond it. Along the coast, Point Loma, Mission Beach, Pacific Beach and La Jolla each have their own pace and housing.

Interstate 5 runs near the coast, with the 805 and the 15 farther inland. The 8 crosses the city through Mission Valley, and the 52 and 56 link the northern neighborhoods. Sorrento Valley and the Torrey Pines Mesa, near UC San Diego, hold many life sciences and tech offices. Kearny Mesa and Mission Valley mix offices, medical buildings and service businesses.

Households here include researchers and engineers paid partly in stock, along with physicians and dentists who own their practices. Others rent out a condo or house near the beach. Some local details are specific to where you live, like your property tax bill from the San Diego County treasurer and the city's short-term rental rules.

Services

Which services do San Diego clients ask us about?

Tax Planning

Nonqualified stock options, often called NSOs, give you the right to buy company shares at a set price. When you exercise them, the gap between that price and the market value is taxed as wages. Planning when to exercise and when to sell can spread that income across tax years.

Read more

Pension and Cash Balance Plans

A practice can set up a cash balance plan, a type of pension with a yearly credit for each person in the plan. Owners in their peak earning years may put away much more this way than with a 401(k) by itself.

Read more

Cost Segregation Study

If you own a rental near the beach or a small commercial building, a cost segregation study may let you deduct parts of it sooner. It places items like appliances, fencing and paving in groups that can be deducted over fewer years than the building.

What do people in San Diego ask us?

How is a stock sale taxed in California compared to federal tax?

Long-term capital gains are profits on assets you held for more than 1 year, and federal tax gives them a lower rate. California taxes those same gains as regular income. A large sale can raise your state tax a lot in one year, so we look at spreading sales over time where it fits.

Do I need a city license to rent my San Diego home for short stays?

The City of San Diego requires a license for most short-term rentals and limits how many whole-home licenses are issued, with a separate limit for Mission Beach. You also collect transient occupancy tax, a city tax on short stays, from your guests. We track that tax with your rental income and expenses so your return matches your records.

Does it matter that you are based in Monrovia and I am in San Diego?

For state income tax, no. California rules are the same in both places, and we work with San Diego clients by video and phone. We do learn your local details, such as your county property tax bill and any city rental license, and include them in your plan.
