What is it like to run a business in West Hollywood?

West Hollywood is a small, walkable city in Los Angeles County, set between Beverly Hills and Hollywood. Santa Monica Boulevard is its main street. The Sunset Strip runs along the north edge below the hills, and Melrose Avenue and Robertson Boulevard hold design showrooms, galleries and boutiques. There is no freeway inside the city, so people get around on surface streets, by bus or on foot.

The local economy depends on hands-on businesses. Restaurants, bars and music venues, salons, fitness studios, boutiques and hotels all compete for customers on a handful of busy streets. Owners deal with cash and card sales, tips, sales tax, rent on a commercial lease and staff schedules that change week to week. Creative workers, talent managers and hospitality workers also live here, often in apartments and condos near Santa Monica Boulevard.

City rules shape money decisions too. West Hollywood has its own rent stabilization law for many older apartments, which limits rent increases and sets rules for landlords. West Hollywood Park and Plummer Park give residents public green space, and the city's busy event calendar brings crowds that local businesses plan around.

Which services does a West Hollywood business usually need?

Accounting and Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping is the steady work of recording every sale, bill and payroll entry. For a restaurant or salon, that includes tracking tips, card fees and the sales tax you collect for the state. Current books make it easier to spot a slow month before it becomes a problem.

CFO Advisory Services

A part-time CFO, or chief financial officer, helps you plan cash, set prices and decide when to hire or sign a new lease. A 13-week cash forecast, which projects money in and out week by week, can show a tight stretch coming.

Strategic Risk Management

A business that serves the public carries liability risk, from a fall at the door to a dispute over a lease. We review your liability and property coverage and what your lease makes you responsible for. We also look at key person coverage, which is insurance that pays the business if an owner or vital employee dies.

Tax Planning

Many owners pay themselves through an S corporation, a company type that passes its profit through to the owner's personal return. Choosing a fair salary, timing equipment purchases and setting up a retirement plan can all change the tax you owe.

Questions

What do people in West Hollywood ask us?

Do you help West Hollywood restaurants with sales tax and tips?

Yes, on the bookkeeping side. We track the sales tax you collect so it matches what you report to the state's Department of Tax and Fee Administration, which collects sales tax. We also record tips carefully in your books. Federal rules on tip income changed in 2025, and California does not automatically follow federal changes, so we check both.

We are registered domestic partners. How does that affect our taxes?

California treats registered domestic partners much like married couples, so you file your state return as a couple. The federal return works differently unless you are also married. Community property rules mean income earned during the partnership is generally owned equally, so you may need to split it on your federal returns.

I own a rent-stabilized building in West Hollywood. Can you help?

Yes. We keep rental income and expenses organized, track depreciation and help you plan for repairs and upgrades. The city's rent rules limit how fast your income can grow, so we build them into your cash plan. We can also check whether a cost segregation study makes sense, while tenant questions belong with an attorney or the city.
