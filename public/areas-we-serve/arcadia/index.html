What is Arcadia like for families and family businesses?

Arcadia lies between Pasadena and Monrovia in Los Angeles County. The 210 crosses the city, and Huntington Drive and Foothill Boulevard both run east into Monrovia. Santa Anita Park and the Los Angeles County Arboretum sit near the corner of Baldwin Avenue and Huntington Drive. The small downtown around First Avenue has a Metro A Line station, and restaurants and shops stretch along Baldwin Avenue, Duarte Road and Live Oak Avenue.

Home life here often spans more than one generation. Grandparents, parents and grown children may share a house, own rental property together or work side by side in a family business. In many older neighborhoods north of Huntington Drive, original ranch homes have given way to larger custom houses. Long-time owners may now hold a home worth far more than they paid. Arcadia County Park and the Arcadia Wilderness Park give families green space close to home.

That kind of family life raises practical questions. Who takes over the business when a parent steps back? How should a house owned by several relatives pass down? How do you stay fair to a child who works in the company and one who does not? Good tax planning and clear conversations help answer each one.

Services

What can 1Oak help an Arcadia family with?

Life Insurance

Life insurance can give a family business the cash to buy out an owner's share after that owner dies, so the company does not have to be sold. It can also even out an inheritance when one child receives the business and another does not.

Accelerated Mortgage Payoff

If you built or bought a larger home, you may wonder whether to pay the loan off early. We compare paying extra each month with keeping that cash for the business or for investing, so you can see the tradeoffs side by side.

Read more

Tax-Free Investment

With a Roth IRA, qualified withdrawals come out free of federal income tax. Moving money from a traditional IRA into one is called a Roth conversion, and it means paying tax now. Under current rules, heirs can usually take money out of an inherited Roth without income tax, which can matter for a family planning across generations.

Read more

Annuities and Rollovers

With an annuity, you hand savings to an insurance company in exchange for income that can last for life, which relies on that insurer's ability to pay claims. For Arcadia retirees who want a steady check alongside Social Security, part of a rollover from a work plan may go into one.

Questions

What do people in Arcadia ask us?

Can my parents and grown children join the same meeting?

Yes. Family meetings often go more smoothly when everyone hears the same plan at the same time. Relatives can sit in the office with you or join by video from wherever they live. We only ask that the person who owns the accounts agrees on who takes part.

Our family owns an Arcadia business together. Where do we start with succession?

Start by writing down who owns what today and who is expected to run the business later. From there we look at what the business is worth, how a buyout could be paid for and what taxes a sale or gift might bring. The terms go into a buy-sell agreement, a contract on how each owner may sell or pass on a share. An attorney drafts it, and we handle the money side.

We own a home with relatives. What happens to the property tax when it passes to the next generation?

California changed its rules in 2021. In many cases a child can keep a parent's lower property tax only if the home becomes the child's main residence. There is also a cap on how much of the low value carries over. We review your situation alongside your estate attorney so you can plan ahead.
