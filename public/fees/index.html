What changes the fee

What affects the cost of the work?

How many returns and entities

A household return costs less to prepare than a household return plus a business return and a trust.

The state of your records

Books that are current take less time than books that are months behind.

One project or ongoing work

A single return or study is priced as a project. Monthly bookkeeping or CFO support is priced as ongoing work.

How many services connect

Planning that covers your taxes, your business and your retirement takes more time than a single question.

Insurance and annuities

How is the firm paid on insurance or annuities?

Insurance and annuity products can be paid for differently from tax and accounting work. Before you decide on any product, ask us how we are paid on it. We will tell you plainly.
