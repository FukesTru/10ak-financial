What does 1Oak Financial Group do?

1Oak Financial Group is a tax, accounting and financial planning firm in Monrovia. We plan and file taxes, keep books, give business owners financial direction, and help households with retirement income, insurance, mortgages and college costs. See all 14 services.

Who do you usually work with?

We focus on business owners and established households, usually between about 35 and 75. Many have income from a business, property or investments, so one decision affects another. That is where having one firm helps most.

Do I have to come to the Monrovia office?

No. You are welcome at our office at 635 W. Foothill Blvd in Monrovia. Clients elsewhere in California usually meet with us by video or phone and share documents electronically.

What happens on the first call?

We ask what prompted you to call, then about your income, your business and your goals. We tell you plainly whether we can help and what the next step would be. You decide whether to go further.

How do your fees work?

Fees depend on the services you need and how complex your situation is. We explain the scope and the fee before any work starts. Read how our fees work.

Can you help if my books or returns are behind?

Yes. Catching up late bookkeeping and filing prior-year returns is common work for us. The sooner you start, the more options you usually have. Learn about tax return filing.
